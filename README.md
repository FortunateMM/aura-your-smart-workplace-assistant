# OmniWork — AI Workplace Productivity Assistant

> **OmniWork is an AI-powered workplace productivity platform, powered by Aura AI, designed to help professionals automate repetitive workplace tasks and work more efficiently.**

## 📌 Project Overview

OmniWork is a single integrated AI workplace productivity platform that brings multiple workplace automation tools into one dashboard.

The application was developed to address common workplace challenges such as repetitive email drafting, processing meeting notes, prioritising tasks, researching topics, and obtaining workplace productivity assistance.

Instead of treating these as separate applications, OmniWork combines them into one cohesive productivity workspace with **Aura AI** as the platform's intelligent assistant.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Professionals spend significant amounts of time on repetitive administrative and communication tasks.

Examples include:

* Writing and rewriting workplace emails
* Processing lengthy meeting notes
* Identifying action items and deadlines
* Prioritising competing tasks
* Researching workplace topics
* Planning daily workloads

These activities can reduce the amount of time available for higher-value work.

OmniWork uses AI-assisted workflows to help reduce this repetitive workload while keeping the human user responsible for reviewing and approving AI-generated outputs.

## 💡 Solution

OmniWork provides one central dashboard containing AI-powered workplace productivity tools.

The platform's AI assistant, **Aura**, supports users across the different workflows while maintaining a consistent professional experience.

## 🧠 Prompt Engineering Approach

A major goal of OmniWork was to demonstrate **structured prompt engineering** rather than relying on simple one-line AI instructions.

The application's AI workflows are designed around a structured prompt approach that considers:

**Role → Context → Task → Tone → Constraints → Output Format → Safety**

### Example structure

**Role**

Define Aura as a professional workplace productivity assistant.

**Context**

Use information supplied by the user to understand the workplace situation.

**Task**

Clearly define what the AI must produce.

**Tone**

Adjust the response according to the user's selected communication style or workplace requirement.

**Constraints**

Prevent the AI from inventing missing information or making unsupported assumptions.

**Output Format**

Require predictable, structured outputs such as summaries, action items, deadlines, schedules, or email sections.

**Safety**

Encourage verification, avoid unsupported claims, and discourage users from entering confidential or sensitive information.

This structured approach helps make the AI outputs more consistent, relevant, and useful.

## 🛡️ Responsible AI

Responsible AI is incorporated into OmniWork through the following principles:

### Human Oversight

AI-generated outputs are intended to assist users rather than replace human judgement.

### Verification

Users are encouraged to review and verify AI-generated content before using it for important workplace decisions.

### Transparency

AI-generated content is clearly identified through messaging such as:

> **Aura-generated · Review before use**

### Privacy

Users are warned not to enter confidential, sensitive, or personal information into the application.

### Accuracy

AI-generated content may contain errors or omissions and should not automatically be treated as verified fact.

### Responsible AI Notice

> AI-generated content may contain errors or omissions. Review and verify AI-generated information before using it for important workplace decisions. Do not enter confidential, sensitive, or personal information.
